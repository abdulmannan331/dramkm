Memory card

Five phases, three beats each. Say the chant out loud while you run Recall mode.

1 · Prep

Angio · Open · Snap
  • Angiogram first: site suitable
  • Peel the foil fully apart
  • Lay out sheath, locator, wire, device
  • Snap locator into sheath, indicators aligned

IFU notes: use within 1 hour of opening. Do not use if the package is damaged or its temperature indicator has darkened.

2 · Locate

Drip · Stop · Drip
  • Wire in, procedure sheath out
  • Blood on, blood off, blood on (≤ 2 cm)
  • Hold sheath still, pull locator + wire

3 · Set

Snap · Pull · Lock
  • Device in until the caps snap
  • Hold sheath, pull device cap straight back
  • Resistance as it snaps into full rear lock
  • Sleeve bands fully visible

4 · Seal

Pull · Tension · Tamp
  • Withdraw along the tract: anchor seats
  • Tamper tube appears at the skin
  • Keep tension until the clear stop shows
  • Tamp to resistance, black marker exposed

5 · Cut

Clear stop, then skin
  • Cut below the clear stop, device off
  • Twirl the tamper tube off
  • Suture up, skin down, cut below the black marker
  • Clean, sterile dressing, check pulses

Know the parts

The kit: insertion sheath, arteriotomy locator, 70 cm guidewire, and the VIP device. Hide the names, then tap a number to check yourself.
